I long time ago I wrote a game for MSDOS where a player had to score a goal from accros a field of starts. The player would shoot an "asteroid" with a certain speed and angle. The stars on the field will atract the asteriod with a certain gravitational pull depending on the mass of the star. The radii of the stars was also different since different types of stars had different densities. The asteriod would be destroyed if it crashes into the star "surface", other wise it would bump on the "walls" of the field until it eventually gets into a small goal on the opposite side of the field. In the rare event of the asteroird stopping it means a no-goal. The asteriord trajectory and speed would change based on the gravitational pull of the starts in the most realistic way.

Different types of starts:
* standard sequence start: medium, low density
* brown dwarf: small, low density
* blue giant: big, medium density
* red giant: big, low density
* black hole: small, high density
